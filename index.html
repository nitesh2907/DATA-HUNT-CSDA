GUIDE
How to play
🗺️ The goal
100 fictional student records are hidden across 10 islands. Each island asks one question that can only be answered by analysing the data. Download the CSV, dig in with Excel, Google Sheets, Python — anything you like — and find the one student who matches the clue.
Step by step
1. Only Island 1 is visible — every other island is hidden in the ☁️ clouds. Click it.
2. Read the mission clue carefully.
3. Press ⬇ Download dataset to get your team's CSV.
4. Find the matching student's Reg_No (e.g. S042).
5. Type it in and press Submit.
6. ✅ Correct clears the clouds from the next island. ❌ Wrong costs 10 points — try again.
Scoring
✅ Correct answer +100 · 💡 Hint −20 (once per island) · ❌ Wrong answer −10 · ⚡ Finish within the first half of the timer +50
⏭ Stuck?
Press Skip for now and that island drifts back into the clouds while the next one is revealed. Skipped islands are listed in amber under the map — reopen them from there before time runs out.
⏱ Timer & submission
You have 60 minutes. When your run ends — treasure found, submitted, or time up — your team, score and time are sent to the organiser automatically. Your progress is saved if the page reloads.
