GUIDE
How to play
🗺️ The goal
The hunt has 3 levels — Easy, Medium and Hard — with 5 questions each. Every level uses a different secret dataset, revealed only when that level opens. Each question points to exactly one row; enter that row's ID.
Step by step
1. Register your team and wait — the organiser starts Level 1 for everyone.
2. Click the glowing island, read the clue, press ⬇ Download dataset.
3. Analyse it in Excel, Google Sheets, Python — anything.
4. Type the ID (e.g. S042, E017, B0123) and press Submit.
5. Correct reveals the next island. Stuck? Skip and come back.
🛑 Between levels
When you finish a level (or its time runs out) the game stops. Wait. The organiser checks the results and opens the next level for the teams that qualified — your screen moves on by itself.
